# 📊 News Sentiment & Stock Market Analysis

## 🧠 Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between financial news sentiment and stock price movements for Nova Financial Solutions. The objective is to explore whether news sentiment can help improve predictive insights for investment decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The goal is to enhance predictive analytics by:
- Extracting sentiment from financial news headlines
- Analyzing stock price movements using technical indicators
- Investigating correlations between sentiment and market behavior
- Supporting data-driven investment strategies

---

## 📊 Work Completed

### Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Analyzed news publication trends over time
- Identified top publishers and their contribution to news volume
- Performed basic text-level exploration of headlines

### Task 2: Stock Data Analysis
- Cleaned and prepared stock price data
- Computed technical indicators:
  - Simple Moving Average (SMA 20, SMA 50)
  - Exponential Moving Average (EMA 12, EMA 26)
  - MACD, Signal line, and Histogram
- Visualized stock trends and indicators

### Task 3: Sentiment Analysis
- Applied NLP-based sentiment scoring on news headlines
- Merged sentiment data with stock market data
- Performed correlation analysis between sentiment and daily stock returns

---

## ⚠️ Notes & Assumptions

- Date alignment was handled between news and stock datasets
- Missing values were cleaned before analysis
- Non-trading day data was adjusted during merging

---

## 🚀 Tools Used

- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- Matplotlib & Seaborn
- Scikit-learn
- TextBlob / VADER (Sentiment Analysis)

---

## 📌 Summary

This project demonstrates how financial news sentiment can be integrated with stock market data to extract meaningful insights that may support investment strategies and predictive analytics.
